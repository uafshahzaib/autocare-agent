# Dashboard Warning Lights (General Reference)

Red icons indicate an issue requiring immediate attention (stop driving safely and check); amber/orange icons indicate an issue that should be addressed soon but is not usually an immediate safety risk; green/blue icons are informational.

Engine warning light (amber, engine outline): indicates the onboard diagnostics system has detected an emissions or engine-management issue. If it is flashing rather than solid, this typically signals an active misfire that can damage the catalytic converter — reduce load and have it inspected promptly.

Oil pressure warning (red, oil can icon): indicates dangerously low oil pressure. Stop the vehicle as soon as safely possible; continuing to drive risks severe engine damage.

Battery/charging warning (red, battery icon): indicates the charging system is not maintaining voltage. The vehicle may run briefly on battery power but can stall unexpectedly.

Brake warning (red, "BRAKE" or exclamation in circle): can indicate low brake fluid, the parking brake is engaged, or an ABS/brake system fault — check parking brake first, then fluid level.

Tire pressure warning (amber, exclamation in horseshoe): indicates one or more tires is significantly under-inflated; check pressures against the recommended values on the door jamb sticker.
