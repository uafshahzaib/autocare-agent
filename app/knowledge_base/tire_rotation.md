# Tire Rotation and Care

Rotate tires every 8,000–10,000 km to promote even tread wear, since front and rear tires wear at different rates depending on drivetrain layout.

Check tire pressure monthly, including the spare; underinflated tires increase fuel consumption and rolling resistance, and raise the risk of blowouts at high speed.

Tread depth should be at least 1.6 mm (legal minimum in most regions); replace before that if performance in wet conditions is a priority, since braking distance increases sharply below 3 mm.

Align wheels whenever new tires are installed, after hitting a significant pothole, or if the vehicle pulls to one side on a flat road.
