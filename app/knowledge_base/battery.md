# 12V Battery Maintenance

A standard lead-acid 12V battery lasts 3-5 years; hot climates and short trips (which don't fully recharge the battery) shorten this lifespan.

Warning signs of a failing battery: slow engine crank, dimming headlights at idle, dashboard battery warning light, or the battery case appearing swollen.

Corrosion on terminals (white/blue crust) increases resistance and should be cleaned with a baking-soda solution; always disconnect the negative terminal first when working on the battery.

If the vehicle has stop-start technology or is a hybrid, it likely uses an AGM (Absorbent Glass Mat) battery, which requires a compatible charger and should only be replaced with the same type.
