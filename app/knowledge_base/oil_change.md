# Engine Oil Change

Recommended interval: every 15,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first, for vehicles using full synthetic oil. For conventional oil, reduce the interval to every 8,000 km.

Signs your oil needs changing sooner: dark, gritty oil on the dipstick; a burning smell; louder-than-usual engine noise; the oil-change dashboard indicator.

Always use the oil viscosity grade specified in the owner's manual (commonly 0W-30 or 5W-30 for modern engines). Using the wrong viscosity can increase engine wear and reduce fuel efficiency.

Skipping oil changes accelerates wear on the timing chain and turbocharger, and can void parts of the powertrain warranty.
