# Brake Service

Brake pads typically last 30,000–70,000 km depending on driving style; front pads wear faster than rear pads because front brakes handle more load during deceleration.

Warning signs: a high-pitched squeal (wear indicator contact), grinding (pads fully worn, metal-on-metal), a soft or spongy pedal (possible air in brake lines or fluid leak), or the car pulling to one side when braking.

Brake fluid should be flushed every 2 years regardless of mileage, since it absorbs moisture over time and its boiling point drops, which can reduce braking performance under heavy use.

Rotors should be inspected at every pad replacement; if they are scored or below minimum thickness, they must be resurfaced or replaced.
